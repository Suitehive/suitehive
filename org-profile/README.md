**Your workspace, on your own server.**

Suitehive is an open-source, self-hostable alternative to Google Workspace: Mail, Calendar and single
sign-on for your team, on your own domain. It speaks the standards your apps already know (IMAP, SMTP,
CalDAV, OpenID Connect) and installs with one command.

We're in **early development**. Images aren't published yet; follow along or help build it.

- **Start here:** [Suitehive/suitehive](https://github.com/Suitehive/suitehive) has the installer, the CLI and the overview
- **How it's designed:** [Suitehive/architecture](https://github.com/Suitehive/architecture)
- **The apps:** [auth](https://github.com/Suitehive/auth) · [mail](https://github.com/Suitehive/mail) · [calendar](https://github.com/Suitehive/calendar)
- **Shared building blocks:** [kit](https://github.com/Suitehive/kit) (Go) · [contracts](https://github.com/Suitehive/contracts) (APIs and events) · [ui](https://github.com/Suitehive/ui) (design system)
- **Running it:** [deploy](https://github.com/Suitehive/deploy)

Next on the roadmap: Contacts, Drive, Docs/Sheets/Slides and Chat.
